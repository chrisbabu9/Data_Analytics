## Phase 4: Portfolio Exposure & Business Insights

### 💡 Hints & Concepts

* **Portfolio Risk Metrics:**
* Total Portfolio Outstanding = $\sum \text{Total Billed Amount}$ across all active accounts.
* At-Risk Outstanding = $\sum \text{Total Billed Amount}$ for accounts in the High-Risk Revolver/Delinquent cluster.


* **Executive Visualization Structure:**
* **Top Level:** KPI Cards (Total Exposure, % Revolvers, Average Portfolio Utilization).
* **Middle Level:** Risk Matrix / Migration Heatmap showing how customers move between payment tiers month-over-month.
* **Bottom Level:** Account-level Drill-down Table filtered by risk tier and utilization thresholds.
